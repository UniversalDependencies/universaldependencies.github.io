Person[psor]: possessor’s person

Values: 1 2 3

Person[psor] is the possessor’s person, marked for exmaple on nouns, emphatic pronouns, infinitives, or adpositions. In possessive uses, such forms typically correspond to an English possessive pronouns followed by a noun.

We do not annotate possessive determiners with Person[psor], as they are actually identical to personal pronouns in the genitive case.

Note that Western Armenian grammars distinguish possessive, demonstrative, and personal articles (suffixes) in addition to the definite article. The same set of suffixes serves all three functions. Depending on the context, they may indicate that the referent is near the speaker (proximal, first person), near the addressee (second person), or distant from both (third person), or they may express possession by one of them. Consequently, the subscript [psor] does not always denote a possessor in this feature, as the same annotation is also used for the person-indexing function of these suffixes. The person-indexing and possessive functions can only be distinguished from context.

See also the related features Number[psor] and Deixis[psor].

1: first person possessor

Examples

  • ձեռքս/jeṙkʼs “my hand” Number=Sing|Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=1
  • ձեռքերս/jeṙkʼers “my hands” Number=Plur|Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=1
  • ըլլալս/ëllals “my being” Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=1
  • մենք՝ հայերս/menkʼ, hayers “we, the Armenians” Number=Plur Person[psor]=1
  • ամենքնիս/amenkʼnis “we all” or “my teacher” Number[psor]=Plur|Person[psor]=1
  • քովս/kʼovs “next to me, by my side” Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=1

2: second person possessor

Examples

  • ձեռքդ/jeṙkʼd “your Sing hand” Number=Sing|Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=2
  • աչքերդ/ačkʼerd “your Sing eyes” Number=Plur|Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=2
  • ըլլալդ/ëllald “your Sing being” Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=2
  • (դուք) բժիշկներդ/(dowkʼ) bžišknerd “you Plur the doctors” or “your doctors” Number=Plur|Person[psor]=2 or Number=Plur|Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=2
  • ինքզինքդ/inkʼzinkʼd “you, Sing yourself” Number=Sing|Person[psor]=2
  • քովդ/kʼovd “next to you, by your Sing side” Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=2

3: third person possessor

Examples

  • աչքերնին/ačkʼernin “their eyes” Number=Plur|Number[psor]=Plur|Person[psor]=3
  • ձեռուընին/jeṙowënin “<btheir</b> hands” Number=Plur|Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=3
  • ինքզինքը/inkʼzinkʼë “he himself, she herself” Number=Sing|Person[psor]=3
  • (նա) ուսուցիչն(ը)/(na) owsowcʼičn(ë) “he/she, the teacher” or “his/her/its teacher” Number=Sing|Person[psor]=3 or Number=Sing|Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=3
  • գալն(ը)/galn(ë) “his/her/its coming” Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=3
  • քովն(ը)/kʼovn(ë) “with him/her/it” Number[psor]=Sing|Person[psor]=3
  • ձեռուընին
  • չգիտնալնուն
  • նոյնինքն

Note that forms containing -ն/-n or -ը/-ë may be ambiguous, since these morphemes are also definite articles, and their function can only be determined from the context (see above).
