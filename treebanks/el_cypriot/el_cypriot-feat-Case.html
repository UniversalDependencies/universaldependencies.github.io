Treebank Statistics: UD_Greek-Cypriot: Features: Case

This feature is universal. It occurs with 4 different values: Acc, Gen, Nom, Voc.

898 tokens (44%) have a non-empty value of Case. 473 types (57%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case. 358 lemmas (59%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case. The feature is used with 7 part-of-speech tags: NOUN (313; 15% instances), DET (300; 15% instances), PRON (149; 7% instances), ADJ (79; 4% instances), PROPN (29; 1% instances), NUM (17; 1% instances), VERB (11; 1% instances).
