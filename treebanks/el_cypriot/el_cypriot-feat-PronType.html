Treebank Statistics: UD_Greek-Cypriot: Features: PronType

This feature is universal. It occurs with 8 different values: Art, Dem, Ind, Int, Neg, Prs, Rel, Tot.

486 tokens (24%) have a non-empty value of PronType. 101 types (12%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of PronType. 43 lemmas (7%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of PronType. The feature is used with 3 part-of-speech tags: DET (303; 15% instances), PRON (150; 7% instances), ADV (33; 2% instances).
