Treebank Statistics: UD_Old_Occitan-CronOc: Features: VerbForm

This feature is universal. It occurs with 3 different values: Fin, Inf, Part.

5974 tokens (16%) have a non-empty value of VerbForm. 1797 types (46%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. 454 lemmas (29%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. The feature is used with 3 part-of-speech tags: VERB (4983; 13% instances), AUX (864; 2% instances), ADJ (127; 0% instances).
