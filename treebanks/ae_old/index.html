Description

UD_Avestan-Old is a manually annotated treebank for Old Avestan and is currently in progress. The corpus consists of religious poetry in the Zoroastrian tradition. Its goals is to cover Gathas 28-34. Currently it contains Gatha 28.

The text is taken from the Corpus Avesticum Berolinense with small adaptations and tagged manually with UPOS and dependency relations. For assigning UPOS, we relied as much as possible on the Old Avestan glossary of Prods Oktor Skjærvø.

Currently it consists of 18 sentences and about 240 words.

Acknowledgments

This was tagged manually by Peter Dirix (KU Leuven) and supported by prof. Velizar Sadvoski (Austrian Academy of Sciences).
