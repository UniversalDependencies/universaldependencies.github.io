Treebank Statistics: UD_Greek-Griko: Features: Case

This feature is universal. It occurs with 4 different values: Acc, Gen, Nom, Voc.

531 tokens (42%) have a non-empty value of Case. 256 types (50%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case. 150 lemmas (50%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case. The feature is used with 7 part-of-speech tags: NOUN (166; 13% instances), DET (158; 13% instances), PRON (138; 11% instances), ADJ (35; 3% instances), NUM (18; 1% instances), PROPN (15; 1% instances), SCONJ (1; 0% instances).
