Treebank Statistics: UD_Pali-PaliCanon: Features: Case

This feature is universal. It occurs with 8 different values: Abl, Acc, Dat, Gen, Ins, Loc, Nom, Voc. Some words have combined values of the feature; 1 combinations have been observed: Gen|Loc.

1196 tokens (58%) have a non-empty value of Case. 409 types (65%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case. 257 lemmas (62%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case.
