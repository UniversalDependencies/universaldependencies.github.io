Treebank Statistics: UD_Lithuanian-ALKSNIS: Features: PronType

This feature is universal but the values Emp are language-specific. It occurs with 8 different values: Dem, Emp, Ind, Int, Neg, Prs, Rel, Tot. Some words have combined values of the feature; 2 combinations have been observed: Ind|Rel, Int|Rel.

4086 tokens (7%) have a non-empty value of PronType. 515 types (3%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of PronType. 73 lemmas (1%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of PronType.
