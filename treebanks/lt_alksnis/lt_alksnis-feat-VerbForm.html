Treebank Statistics: UD_Lithuanian-ALKSNIS: Features: VerbForm

This feature is universal but the values Conv are language-specific. It occurs with 5 different values: Conv, Fin, Ger, Inf, Part.

9025 tokens (16%) have a non-empty value of VerbForm. 5172 types (32%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. 2201 lemmas (27%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm.
