Treebank Statistics: UD_Lithuanian-ALKSNIS: Features: Tense

This feature is universal. It occurs with 3 different values: Fut, Past, Pres.

6576 tokens (12%) have a non-empty value of Tense. 3894 types (24%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Tense. 1781 lemmas (22%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Tense. The feature is used with 2 part-of-speech tags: VERB (5953; 11% instances), AUX (623; 1% instances).

VERB

5953 VERB tokens (72% of all VERB tokens) have a non-empty value of Tense.

The most frequent other feature values with which VERB and Tense co-occurred: Reflex=EMPTY (5242; 88%), Case=EMPTY (3845; 65%), Gender=EMPTY (3588; 60%), Voice=EMPTY (3588; 60%), Mood=Ind (3234; 54%), VerbForm=Fin (3234; 54%), Number=EMPTY (3145; 53%).

VERB tokens may have the following values of Tense:

  • Fut (323; 5% of non-empty Tense): reikės, liks, nereikės, teks, tiks, turės, beliks, galėsi, ims, išvengsite
  • Past (2398; 40% of non-empty Tense): sakė, nustatyta, liko, likus, planuota, susiję, atsirado, pateikti, teigė, turėjo
  • Pres (3232; 54% of non-empty Tense): gali, turi, reikia, yra, rodo, nėra, galite, atrodo, privalo, tampa
  • EMPTY (2305): turėtų, pateikti, dirbti, sukurti, teikti, parengti, užtikrinti, padaryti, padėti, reikėtų
