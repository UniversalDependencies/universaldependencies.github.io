Treebank Statistics: UD_Lithuanian-ALKSNIS: Features: Case

This feature is universal. It occurs with 8 different values: Acc, Dat, Gen, Ill, Ins, Loc, Nom, Voc.

29273 tokens (52%) have a non-empty value of Case. 12353 types (76%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case. 5656 lemmas (70%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case.
