Treebank Statistics: UD_Ottoman_Turkish-DUDU: Features: VerbForm

This feature is universal but the values Conv, Vnoun are language-specific. It occurs with 4 different values: Conv, Fin, Part, Vnoun.

5058 tokens (20%) have a non-empty value of VerbForm. 1903 types (21%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. 497 lemmas (11%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. The feature is used with 2 part-of-speech tags: VERB (5014; 20% instances), AUX (44; 0% instances).
