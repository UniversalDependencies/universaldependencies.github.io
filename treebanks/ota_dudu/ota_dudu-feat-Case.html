Treebank Statistics: UD_Ottoman_Turkish-DUDU: Features: Case

This feature is universal but the values Equ are language-specific. It occurs with 8 different values: Abl, Acc, Dat, Equ, Gen, Ins, Loc, Nom.

12886 tokens (52%) have a non-empty value of Case. 6472 types (71%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case. 3540 lemmas (75%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case. The feature is used with 7 part-of-speech tags: NOUN (9176; 37% instances), PROPN (1876; 8% instances), PRON (1250; 5% instances), VERB (316; 1% instances), ADJ (217; 1% instances), NUM (43; 0% instances), AUX (8; 0% instances).
