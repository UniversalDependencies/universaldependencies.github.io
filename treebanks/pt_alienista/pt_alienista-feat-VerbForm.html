Treebank Statistics: UD_Portuguese-Alienista: Features: VerbForm

This feature is universal. It occurs with 4 different values: Fin, Ger, Inf, Part.

2929 tokens (14%) have a non-empty value of VerbForm. 1547 types (39%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. 756 lemmas (27%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. The feature is used with 3 part-of-speech tags: VERB (2269; 11% instances), AUX (512; 2% instances), ADJ (148; 1% instances).
