Treebank Statistics: UD_Dargwa-Mehweb: Features: Mood

This feature is universal but the values App, Prh are language-specific. It occurs with 6 different values: App, Cnd, Imp, Ind, Opt, Prh. Some words have combined values of the feature; 3 combinations have been observed: App|Cnd, Cnd|Opt, Imp|Prh.

785 tokens (18%) have a non-empty value of Mood. 389 types (29%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Mood. 126 lemmas (24%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Mood.
