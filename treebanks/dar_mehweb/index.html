Description

UD Mehweb Dargwa is a Universal Dependencies (UD) treebank for the Dargwa Mehweb lect. The annotation was converted from interlinear glossed text and manually annotated for syntactic relations. The treebank includes a selection of sentences that presents oral conversation, oral folktales, Pear Stories, elicited examples, grammar examples. The treebank contains approximately 4,400 tokens.

Mehweb is a north-central lect of Dargwa languages spoken in East Caucasus. Dargwa Mehweb is spoken in the village Megeb (Republic of Daghestan, Russian Federation). The language is spoken by approximately 800 people (Dobrushina et al. 2017). Megeb is the only Dargwa village in the area surrounded by Lak and Avar villages. The official language of the village is Avar, children are taught Avar in school. Most of the speakers are bi- or trilingual (Kozhukhar & Barylnikova 2013).

The treebank consists of examples collected in (Daniel et al. 2019). The sentences are written in IPA-based orthography developed during the field trips in 2010s and provided with glosses based on Leipzig Glossing Rules and English translations.

The treebank includes a selection of sentences that presents oral conversation, oral folktales, Pear Stories, elicited examples, grammar examples. The treebank contains approximately 4,400 tokens. Most data were recorded during field trips to Megeb in 2013-2016. In addition, several sentences are taken from Magometov’s 1982 texts. The treebank presents oral data such as personal conversation, oral folktales, Pear Stories, elicited examples, grammar examples. The treebank contains approximately 4,400 tokens.

The sentences were converted from interlinear glossed text into CoNLL-U format using a custom conversion script (with minor manual corrections). The syntactic relations were subsequently manually annotated following the UD framework. A pilot lemma annotation was added using DarLing and the Dictionary of the Mehweb language.
