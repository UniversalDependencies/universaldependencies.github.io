Description

A treebank for Middle Georgian, containig morphosyntactic annotations of Amirandarejaniani, V (XII cent.) and Rusudaniani, III (XVII cent.).

UD_Old_Georgian-MidGLauro is a treebank based on the novels Amirandarejaniani, V (XII cent.) and Rusudaniani, III (XVII cent.). The text is retrieved from GNC. The sentences are annotated through a semi-automated workflow: UD_Old_Georgian-OGLauRo is used as few-shot examples, together with additional guidelines specific for Middle Georgian and Ilia Abuladze’s ძველი ქართული ენის ლექსიკონი (მასალები), to prompt Large Language Models (LLMs) to prepare a draft annotation, which is then corrected manually.
