Description

UD Gorani-Hawrami is a preliminary conversion of one spoken Hewramî narrative, ZB (zaroɫe û bizê, “The baby and the goat”), from Masoud Mohammadirad’s published Hewramî recordings and glossed text.

This is the first Universal Dependencies treebank of Gorani (ISO 639-3: hac), in the Hawrami / Hewramî variety. The current release contains a single complete oral narrative, kept in source order rather than shuffled. Sentence ids hwr-rec-zb-001–hwr-rec-zb-061 correspond one-to-one to source annotation units ZB.1–ZB.61. Those units are intonation units from the source recording, so one UD sentence may contain more than one clause.

The underlying text and interlinear morphology come from:

  • Masoud Mohammadirad, A corpus of Hewramî recordings, time-aligned with transcription and translation (2025), Zenodo, 10.5281/zenodo.15419952. Audio timing follows the ELAN alignment in that archive. The archive is not redistributed here.
  • Masoud Mohammadirad, Echoes of the past: Hewramî narratives (Language Science Press, 2025), 10.5281/zenodo.17140764. This published glossed edition is the canonical transcription and the source of the English translation. It is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Persian translations (text_fa) were added in the Hawrami corpus project and still need human review. Lemmas and UD tags are mapped from the published interlinear glossing and glossary. Dependency trees are a machine-assisted first conversion. The CoNLL-U comments mark the layer as preliminary silver data; it is not gold annotation.

The treebank has 61 sentences and about 520 words, below the threshold for a train/dev/test split. All sentences are in hac_hawrami-ud-test.conllu. There is no separate training or development file.
