Treebank Statistics: UD_Polish-MPDT: Features: VerbForm

This feature is universal but the values Conv, Vnoun are language-specific. It occurs with 5 different values: Conv, Fin, Inf, Part, Vnoun.

8912 tokens (15%) have a non-empty value of VerbForm. 5253 types (33%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. 2242 lemmas (26%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. The feature is used with 4 part-of-speech tags: VERB (6506; 11% instances), ADJ (1055; 2% instances), AUX (845; 1% instances), NOUN (506; 1% instances).

6506 VERB tokens (100% of all VERB tokens) have a non-empty value of VerbForm.
