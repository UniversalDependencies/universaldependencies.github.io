Treebank Statistics: UD_Serbian-ParCoLab: Features: Tense

This feature is universal. It occurs with 4 different values: Fut, Imp, Past, Pres.

5588 tokens (12%) have a non-empty value of Tense. 1994 types (16%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Tense. 1223 lemmas (17%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Tense. The feature is used with 2 part-of-speech tags: VERB (2858; 6% instances), AUX (2730; 6% instances).

2858 VERB tokens (51% of all VERB tokens) have a non-empty value of Tense.

The most frequent other feature values with which VERB and Tense co-occurred: Voice=EMPTY (2858; 100%), Gender=EMPTY (2737; 96%), Mood=Ind (2483; 87%), VerbForm=Fin (2483; 87%), Person=3 (1768; 62%), Number=Sing (1687; 59%).
