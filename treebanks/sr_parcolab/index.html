Description

ParCoLab is a treebank of Serbian based on literary texts. It was originally developed between 2014 and 2018 (original corpus available here).

ParCoLab is based on two works of fiction: “Bašta, pepeo” by Danilo Kiš and “Testament” by Vidosav Stevanović. It was originally annotated in a language-specific annotation scheme (original corpus available here). The conversion to UD was done semi-automatically, using conversion scripts, followed by extensive manual checks and corrections. The corpus was developed as part of the ParCoLab project.
