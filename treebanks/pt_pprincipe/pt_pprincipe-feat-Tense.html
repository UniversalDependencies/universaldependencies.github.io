Treebank Statistics: UD_Portuguese-PPrincipe: Features: Tense

This feature is universal. It occurs with 5 different values: Fut, Imp, Past, Pqp, Pres.

2178 tokens (13%) have a non-empty value of Tense. 944 types (37%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Tense. 362 lemmas (22%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Tense. The feature is used with 2 part-of-speech tags: VERB (1621; 10% instances), AUX (557; 3% instances).
