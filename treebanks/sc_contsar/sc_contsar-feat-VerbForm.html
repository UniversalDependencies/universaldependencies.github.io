Treebank Statistics: UD_Sardinian-ContSar: Features: VerbForm

This feature is universal. It occurs with 4 different values: Fin, Ger, Inf, Part.

1103 tokens (14%) have a non-empty value of VerbForm. 602 types (28%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. 325 lemmas (20%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. The feature is used with 2 part-of-speech tags: VERB (728; 9% instances), AUX (375; 5% instances).
