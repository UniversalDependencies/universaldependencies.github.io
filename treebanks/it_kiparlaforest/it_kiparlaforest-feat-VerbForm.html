Treebank Statistics: UD_Italian-KIParlaForest: Features: VerbForm

This feature is universal. It occurs with 4 different values: Fin, Ger, Inf, Part.

4405 tokens (18%) have a non-empty value of VerbForm. 1016 types (29%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. 357 lemmas (14%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. The feature is used with 2 part-of-speech tags: VERB (3041; 12% instances), AUX (1364; 6% instances).

3041 VERB tokens (97% of all VERB tokens) have a non-empty value of VerbForm.
