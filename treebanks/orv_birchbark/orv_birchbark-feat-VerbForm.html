Treebank Statistics: UD_Old_East_Slavic-Birchbark: Features: VerbForm

This feature is universal but the values PartRes are language-specific. It occurs with 4 different values: Fin, Inf, Part, PartRes.

2774 tokens (9%) have a non-empty value of VerbForm. 2128 types (17%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. 693 lemmas (17%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. The feature is used with 2 part-of-speech tags: VERB (2678; 9% instances), AUX (96; 0% instances).
