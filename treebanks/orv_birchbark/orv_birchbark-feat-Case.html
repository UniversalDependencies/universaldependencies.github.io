Treebank Statistics: UD_Old_East_Slavic-Birchbark: Features: Case

This feature is universal. It occurs with 7 different values: Acc, Dat, Gen, Ins, Loc, Nom, Voc. Some words have combined values of the feature; 2 combinations have been observed: Acc|Gen, Acc|Nom.

12029 tokens (41%) have a non-empty value of Case. 8114 types (66%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case. 3210 lemmas (76%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case.
