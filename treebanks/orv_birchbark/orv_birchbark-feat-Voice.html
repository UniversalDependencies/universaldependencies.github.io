Treebank Statistics: UD_Old_East_Slavic-Birchbark: Features: Voice

This feature is universal but the values Mid are language-specific. It occurs with 3 different values: Act, Mid, Pass.

2792 tokens (9%) have a non-empty value of Voice. 2146 types (18%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Voice. 699 lemmas (17%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Voice. The feature is used with 2 part-of-speech tags: VERB (2698; 9% instances), AUX (94; 0% instances).

2698 VERB tokens (98% of all VERB tokens) have a non-empty value of Voice.
