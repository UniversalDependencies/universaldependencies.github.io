Treebank Statistics: UD_Ukrainian-Zhelekhivka: Features: PronType

This feature is universal but the values Emp are language-specific. It occurs with 8 different values: Dem, Emp, Ind, Int, Neg, Prs, Rel, Tot.

1156 tokens (12%) have a non-empty value of PronType. 272 types (9%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of PronType. 89 lemmas (4%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of PronType. The feature is used with 4 part-of-speech tags: PRON (568; 6% instances), DET (456; 5% instances), ADV (110; 1% instances), SCONJ (22; 0% instances).
