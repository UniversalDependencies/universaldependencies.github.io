Treebank Statistics: UD_Ukrainian-Zhelekhivka: Features: Gender

This feature is universal. It occurs with 3 different values: Fem, Masc, Neut.

3798 tokens (41%) have a non-empty value of Gender. 2074 types (67%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Gender. 1255 lemmas (62%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Gender.
