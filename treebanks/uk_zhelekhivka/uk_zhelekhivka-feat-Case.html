Treebank Statistics: UD_Ukrainian-Zhelekhivka: Features: Case

This feature is universal. It occurs with 7 different values: Acc, Dat, Gen, Ins, Loc, Nom, Voc.

4979 tokens (53%) have a non-empty value of Case. 2289 types (74%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case. 1312 lemmas (65%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Case.
