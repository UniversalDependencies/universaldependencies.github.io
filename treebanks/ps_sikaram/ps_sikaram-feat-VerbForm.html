Treebank Statistics: UD_Pashto-Sikaram: Features: VerbForm

This feature is universal but the values Vnoun are language-specific. It occurs with 4 different values: Fin, Inf, Part, Vnoun.

880 tokens (13%) have a non-empty value of VerbForm. 247 types (16%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. 131 lemmas (11%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of VerbForm. The feature is used with 4 part-of-speech tags: VERB (547; 8% instances), AUX (283; 4% instances), NOUN (39; 1% instances), ADJ (11; 0% instances).
