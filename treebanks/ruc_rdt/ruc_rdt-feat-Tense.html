Treebank Statistics: UD_Ruuli-RDT: Features: Tense

This feature is universal. It occurs with 4 different values: Fut, Nar, Past, Pres.

1642 tokens (20%) have a non-empty value of Tense. 1302 types (44%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Tense. 357 lemmas (25%) occur at least once with a non-empty value of Tense. The feature is used with 2 part-of-speech tags: VERB (1359; 17% instances), AUX (283; 3% instances).
