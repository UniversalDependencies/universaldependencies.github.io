ConvType: converb type

Converb types distinguish temporal converbs and converbs expressing logical relations.

Imm: immediate

The immediate anterior converb encodes an event which immediately precedes the event in the matrix clause.

Examples

  • w-ak’-a-rijal : M-come:PFV-IRR-IMM “just after he came”

Purp: purposive

Examples

  • hil-d-ix-alis : PV-F1-lie.down:PFV-PURP “in order for her to go to sleep”

Cond: conditional

Examples

  • g-a-k’a : give:PFV-IRR-COND “if (s)he gave”
  • nu, di-la urši-li-ni xunul k-a-k’a, iχ-di-li-šu-r d-uʔ-es-i : I I.OBL-GEN boy-OBL-ERG wife bring:PFV-IRR-COND that-PL-OBL-AD-HPL(ESS) F1-be-INF-ATR “If my son marries, I will live at their place”

Conc: concessive

Examples

  • ħa-g-a-k’ara : NEG-give:PFV-IRR-CONC “though (s)he did not give”

Post: posterior

Examples

  • b-ak’-ače : HPL-come:PFV-PSTR “before they came”

Smlt: simultaneous

Examples

  • b-elč’-un-ijadal : N-read:PFV-AOR-SMLT “as soon as (s)he reads”

Cntf: counterfactual

Examples

  • w-aq”-un-q’alle : M-do:PFV-AOR-CTRF “if he had done”

Incp: inceptive

Examples

  • w-ak’-ib-i-čela : M-come:PFV-AOR-PTCP-INCP “since the moment he came”

Loc: locative

Locative is weakly attested in Mehweb.

Examples

  • b-ix-ib-i-ħe-w : N-put:PFV-AOR-PTCP-IN-M(ESS) “where I put it”

Ant: anteriority

Examples

  • b-ak’-ib-i-ʁale : HPL-come:PFV-AOR-PTCP-ANTE “when (after) they came”

Grad: gradual

Examples

  • b-elč-un-i-cad(i) : N-read:PFV-AOR-PTCP-GRAD “the more (s)he has read”

Caus: causal

Examples

  • _d-ak’-i-le-na : F1-come:PFV-AOR-CVB-CAUSAL “because she came”

Conc2: concessive

Second concessive converb is used when the event described by the converbal clause was unlikely to happen and probably undesirable; but since it nevertheless did happen, the action in the main clause takes place. So its meaning is close to concession and causality.

Examples

  • ħu w-ak’-i-leʡur, nuša-ni ħa-d ʡoˤχlad-deš d-aq’-iša : you.sg(NOM) M-come:PFV-AOR-PTCP?-CONC2 we-ERG you-DAT hospitable-NMLZ NPL-do:PFV-FUT.EGO “As you have come here, we will show you hospitality.”
