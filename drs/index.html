UD for Gedeo

Tokenization and Word Segmentation

  • The underlying text is provided in a Latin-based writing system (not in the Ethiopic script).
  • In general, surface tokens (orthographic words) are separated by whitespace.

Morphology

Gedeo is morphologically a complex language. It is mostly a suffixing language. Nominals in Gedeo inflect for number, gender, and case. They inflect for nine cases and also mark singulative which give rise for the notion of definiteness or specificity. The verbs inflect for person number, tense/aspect, and mood. A fully inflected declarative verb may inflect for perfective, perfect and imperfective forms. The latter include the present and past progressive, as well as future forms. The number of underived adjectives in Gedeo is few. Like nouns adjectives distinguish case, gender and number. They share grammatical features with nouns.

Tags

  • Gedeo currently uses 13 of the 17 universal POS categories, including particles (PART). The AUX, SCONJ, SYM, X categories do not occur in the present data.

Nominal Features

  • The two values of the Number feature are Sing and Plur.
  • Case: Nom, Abs, Gen, Dat.

Verbal Features

  • There is no Tense feature but there is Aspect with two values: Imp and Perf.

Syntax

The constituent order is SOV/OV. A simple sentence is formed of a single NP subject and a predicate VP. A noun phrase consists minimally of a head nominal: a common noun, a pronoun or a proper name. Modifiers are optional constituents of an NP, which can be any of the following: demonstratives, numerals, quantifiers, and nominal attributives. A simple verb phrase (VP) is formed of a simple verb which can be expanded by adding modifying elements such as adverbs and other modifiers. Simple sentences are often categorized into declarative, interrogative, and imperative. A declarative sentence can be classified as eventive/stative, copular, possessive, and existential.
