obl:agent: oblique agent in a passive construction

The obl:agent relation marks the agent of a passive predicate, usually introduced by da “by”. This agent is the subject of the corresponding active clause.

1	a	_	DET	_	_	2	det	_	Gloss=the
2	deçixon	_	NOUN	_	_	7	nsubj:pass	_	Gloss=decision
3	a	_	PRON	_	_	7	expl	_	Gloss=it
4	l’	_	PART	_	_	7	dep	_	SpaceAfter=No

Oblique complements of adjectives instead take obl:arg.

Sources and instruments introduced by da take obl as modifiers or obl:arg as selected arguments.

In non-passive causative or permissive constructions, an oblique causee takes obl:arg. In a fa lavâ a machina da seu maio “she has her husband wash the car”, lavâ takes ccomp because its actor is expressed separately by da seu maio, which attaches to the infinitive with obl:arg.
